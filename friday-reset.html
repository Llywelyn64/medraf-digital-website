What to do with your briefing

  1. Ask follow-up questions. It's a normal chat, so you can keep going. For example: “Help me break my first priority into steps”, or “Draft the email I've been putting off.”
  2. Keep it. Copy the briefing into the same document as your answers, so it's there on Monday morning.
  3. Start Monday with your first action, before you open your inbox.
  4. Look back after a month. Paste in four weeks of briefings and ask what patterns it can see.

Make it a habit

Set aside the same twenty minutes every Friday. After a month, look back. You'll begin to see what consistently moves forward and what keeps getting stuck.
