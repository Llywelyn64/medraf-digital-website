Does December look like this?

You mean to post properly this Christmas. Then December fills up, the way it always does. Evenings go on working out what to say, finding a picture and getting it posted, and some days it simply doesn’t happen.

None of that is a failing. It is a planning job, and nobody has done it for you yet.

Why I created this

I know just how hectic December can be.

When I was teaching, the working day rarely ended when I left school. There were lessons to prepare, books to mark and a hundred little things to organise for the following day.

And then there was Christmas itself. School Christmas concerts, Christmas fairs, shopping lists, cards to write, presents to wrap, baking, visiting relatives and trying to find those precious moments to make memories with the people who matter most.

I love Christmas, but there never seemed to be enough hours in the day.

And that’s why I created this bundle.

Because when you’re running a small business, your work doesn’t stop just because Christmas is approaching. But your time is precious, and you shouldn’t have to spend every evening wondering what to post on social media.

If this bundle takes even a little of that pressure away and gives you more time to enjoy December, then I’ve achieved what I set out to do.

How it works

  1. In November, open your 25 posts

    Open the Canva template and click Use template. It becomes your own copy to change, and a short guide walks you through every step.

  2. Make them yours

    Change the colours, swap in your own photos and fill in the brackets in each caption. Then print your posters and counter card.

  3. From 1 December, one email each morning

    A reminder of which picture and which caption to use that day. You post your own picture and your filled-in caption, or schedule them ahead.

  • Five A4 posters for your window or counter, each with a space for a QR code

  • A short step-by-step guide, with Canva quick help and ten Christmas colour palettes
  • Optional prompts for making your own Christmas pictures with ChatGPT or Gemini

  • Two editions, both included: a Products edition that follows a florist, and a Services edition that follows a grooming parlour. Use whichever fits your business

Is this bundle right for you?

Whether you run a little shop, a busy salon or a service-based business, Christmas marketing can easily slip to the bottom of your to-do list.

This bundle is for small business owners who want to:

  • Stay visible throughout December without planning every post from scratch.
  • Create festive content that reflects their own business and personality.
  • Promote their products, services or Christmas offers.
  • Spend less time worrying about marketing and more time enjoying the season.

You don’t need to be a marketing expert. Just bring your business knowledge and a little creativity.

A few things to know before you buy

It’s a DIY bundle, with the planning already done.

You’ll receive 25 ready-to-personalise posts with their captions, posters, a counter card, a posting calendar and step-by-step guidance.

You’ll need:

  • A free or paid Canva account for the templates.
  • Photos of your own work, products or premises. A phone is fine.
  • A little time before December to personalise and prepare your content.

If you would also like to make AI pictures, a free ChatGPT or Gemini account is enough. Free limits vary, so make a few at a time. We recommend preparing your posts in November so there’s plenty of time.

The bundle is available to buyers in the UK, under our Terms of Sale.

Nothing is personalised automatically, but you’ll have clear instructions and worked examples to guide you.

Questions

Do I need to pay for any tools?

No. The templates work on a free Canva account. If you want to make AI pictures too, a free ChatGPT or Gemini account is enough.

Am I technical enough?

If you can copy and paste, and send an email, you can do this. Every step is written out for you.

Does it work if I offer a service?

Yes. There are two editions, one for businesses that sell things and one for businesses that offer a service, and you get both.

How long will it take?

That depends on you, and on how much you change. You can work through it in your own time during November.

Do I have to post every day?

No. There’s a post for every day from 1 to 24 December and one for January, but you can post all of them or just the ones that suit you, and pick it up again any day.

How does it arrive?

Straight away. After you pay, you’ll land on a page with links to both editions in Google Drive. Each folder opens with a short page listing the Canva template links, then the guide (as a PDF, a Word file and a Google Doc you can type into), the planner and the calendar.

Can I get a VAT invoice?

Yes. Medraf Digital Limited is VAT registered (VAT number 523 7259 88), and the £49 includes VAT at 20%, which is £8.17. An invoice showing our VAT number is made automatically when you pay, and you can download it straight away. If you have any trouble, email hello@medrafdigital.co.uk.

Can I buy from outside the UK?

Not at the moment. The bundle is available to buyers in the UK only, which keeps the price and VAT simple for everyone. We hope to welcome buyers from further afield in future.

When does it close?

On 30 November, so that everyone starts posting together on 1 December.
