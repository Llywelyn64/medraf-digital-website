AI Presence Audit + Training

You know your business is there.
But can everybody else find it and understand it?

When you look at your own business online, you know exactly what you're looking for.

You recognise your name, your website, your profiles and your services. You already know how all the pieces fit together.

Someone discovering your business for the first time doesn't.

And neither does AI.

It has to work with the information it can find.

Sometimes a business is difficult to distinguish from other people or companies with similar names.

Sometimes it can be found, but different places describe the business in different ways.

Sometimes the information is there, but it isn't clear what the business actually does, who it helps or what somebody should do next.

And sometimes everything looks perfectly clear from inside the business while the outside picture tells a different story.

That's what the AI Presence Audit is designed to uncover.

The audit looks at what is actually there.

The AI Presence Audit looks at your business from the outside.

I investigate what can actually be found and how clearly your business can be identified and understood.

I look for inconsistencies, gaps and areas of uncertainty.

I look at what somebody encounters as they move from discovering your business towards deciding whether to take a next step.

And I look at what AI appears to understand from the information available to it.

The purpose isn't to find somewhere to insert AI for the sake of it.

It's to see the picture that is difficult to see when you're standing inside your own business.

Being online doesn't necessarily mean being understood

Two businesses can have completely different presence problems.

  • One might appear in plenty of places, but describe itself differently in each one.
  • Another might know exactly what it does, but struggle to appear clearly amongst other people or businesses with similar names.
  • Another might be easy to find but leave somebody unsure about what it offers or what to do next.

There isn't one predetermined problem I'm looking for.

The audit is there to find out what's actually happening in your business.

And then we do something with what we find.

I don't want to hand you a report full of observations and leave you wondering what to do with them.

Your findings shape the next part of the work: relevant AI training.

Not a generic introduction to AI.

Not a tour of whichever tools happen to be fashionable.

And not a demonstration where I do everything while you watch.

We work on the things that are relevant to you, your business and what the audit has uncovered.

That could include learning how to give AI better context, create clearer and more consistent information, develop useful prompts for work you repeatedly do, question and improve an AI response, or understand where AI is useful and where human judgement still belongs.

The exact training depends on what we find.

You do it with me, so you can do it without me.

My background is in teaching, and that shapes the way I work.

I don't want to show you something clever and send you away hoping you remember it.

Wherever possible, you do the work during the training.

You try it.
You question it.
You change it when it doesn't sound like you.

And you learn how to do it again when I'm not sitting beside you.

The aim is not dependence on Medraf Digital.

It's greater understanding, confidence and control.

What changes afterwards?

  • You understand more clearly how your business appears from the outside.
  • You know where information is clear and consistent and where it isn't.
  • You have a better understanding of what AI can and cannot make sense of about your business.
  • You know which gaps deserve your attention rather than trying to fix everything at once.
  • And you've had practical AI training shaped around the things the investigation actually uncovered.

So instead of leaving with a generic list of AI tools to try, you leave knowing more about your own business, what needs attention and what you can do next.
