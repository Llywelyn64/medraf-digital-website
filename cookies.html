Medraf Digital Limited Cookie Policy

Hello there!

Thank you for checking out our cookie policy. We are committed to ensuring that our website and its use of cookies and other technologies comply with UK laws on privacy and data protection. This policy sets out the types of cookies and similar technologies we use, the exact cookies used on our website, whether we are using any third-party cookies, how you can control our use of cookies, what their expiry dates are and how you can get in contact with us for more information.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files of information, usually containing letters and numbers, that websites store on your browser, or the hard drive of your computer. Strictly necessary cookies do not require your consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), as they are essential for the operation of our website or to provide a service you have requested. Where these cookies involve personal data, we rely on our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR.

All other cookies and similar technologies are only placed on your device where you have chosen to use the feature that sets them. This website does not display a cookie banner, because it sets no cookies of its own and places nothing on your device unless you open and use the chat assistant.

We do not use cookies to analyse how visitors use this website, and we do not use cookies to distinguish one visitor from another.

Do you use similar technologies?

We also use similar technologies such as session storage, which works in a similar way to cookies and stores information on your device for the length of your visit. The items used on this website are listed below, and none of them is placed until you choose to use the chat assistant.

What type of cookies do you use?

We use the following cookie types:

  • Essential cookies. These can also be known as strictly necessary cookies. They are needed for a website to work at all, for example to spread traffic across servers without slowing the site down. We do not use any essential cookies on this website.
  • Analytical cookies. These can also be known as performance cookies, and they gather information about how visitors use a website, so the site owner can understand what works well and what does not. We do not use analytical cookies on this website.
  • Functionality cookies. These make your experience on a website easier by remembering preferences and simplifying interactions, for example remembering a conversation you have already begun. The chat assistant described below sets one item of this kind, and only once you have chosen to use it.
  • Advertising cookies. These can also be known as targeting cookies. They track your visits across a website and the links you follow, so that adverts can be shown based on what has been learned about you. We do not use advertising cookies on this website, and this website carries no advertising.

Where you use our chat assistant, information about that conversation is processed by its provider, Dante AI, on our behalf, under a data processing agreement with them.

We do not control how that provider uses data beyond that agreement, and we recommend reading their own privacy and cookie policies. Where data is transferred outside the UK, appropriate safeguards are in place.

We’ve provided a detailed breakdown below of all the cookies and other technologies we use on our website, and why we use them.

We do not set any first-party cookies. This website is made up of plain web pages and does not use a website builder, advertising network or analytics tool. The only items described below are set by our chat assistant, and only where you choose to use it.

Do you use any third-party cookies?

Third party cookies are not set by us, but are set by other domains than the website you visit. These cookies are placed on website visitors’ devices by third-party outlets. Common examples of these third parties can include social media platforms, analytical providers, and advertising networks.

We use one third-party provider that places an item on your device: Dante AI, which provides the chat assistant on this website.

We do not control the use of cookies by third parties and recommend reviewing their respective privacy and cookie policies for further information.

Some third-party providers may transfer your data outside the UK. Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK data protection laws.

How can I control these cookies?

Nothing is placed on your device unless you open the chat assistant and send a message. If you would prefer nothing to be stored, simply do not use the chat assistant. The rest of the website works exactly the same either way.

If you have already used the chat assistant and want to remove what it has stored, you can clear your browser’s cookies and site data for dante-ai.com. The session storage items listed above clear by themselves as soon as you close the tab.

The steps differ slightly between browsers, and are found under Settings, then Privacy, then cookies and site data. You may also set your browser to block or delete cookies generally.

Because we set no cookies of our own and nothing is placed on your device before you choose to use the chat assistant, this website does not display a cookie banner or a cookie settings control.

Please note:

  • Choosing not to use the chat assistant does not affect any other part of this website.
  • There are no strictly necessary cookies on this website to disable.

You may also adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. However, blocking essential cookies via your browser may impact website functionality.

What is the expiry period for your cookies?

Each cookie’s duration is set out in the table above. Some cookies are session cookies (which expire when you close your browser), and others are persistent cookies (which remain on your device for a defined period).

You may also adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies generally.
