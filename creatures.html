Smart Creatures

The Smart Creatures.

Smart Creatures is a Medraf Digital story series. Each one follows a different creature running a different kind of business, and the everyday pressures that quietly build up behind the scenes.

They are not real, but the problems they face are ones many businesses will recognise. The enquiry nobody answers. The message that gets buried. The same piece of admin, done again and again.

The first is Ambrose.

More Smart Creatures are on their way.

Ambrose is the first story from the Smart Creatures world. More characters, businesses and stories will appear here over time.

Smart Creatures explore the kinds of problems Medraf Digital looks at: where people get stuck, where work is repeated or missed, and where technology could genuinely help.

Sometimes the answer is AI. Sometimes it isn’t.
